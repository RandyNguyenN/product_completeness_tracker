Frequently Asked Questions

Which fields are checked?
Five fields on the product: Product Name, Sales Description, Product Category, Product Image and Sale Price. Each one is worth 20%.
Why is a product with a price of 0 not complete?
The sale price only counts as filled when it is greater than 0, so products accidentally left at 0 are flagged.
How is the Product Category counted?
In Odoo 17 every product has a category (Odoo sets "All" by default), so this check is always met.
Where do I see the score?
In the list view of Products (the columns Completeness and Missing Fields). Switch the product view from kanban to list to see them.
Do I need to recompute anything after install?
No. Scores are computed for existing products at install and update automatically whenever one of the five fields changes.
Can I choose other fields or weights?
Not in this version: the five fields and equal weights are fixed, which keeps the module simple and configuration-free.
